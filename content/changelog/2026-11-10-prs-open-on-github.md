---
title: The queue page shows the queue, and PRs open on GitHub
kind: improved
---

The **Queue** page lists each queue in the order it merges, with every PR's stage and what holds
it. Click a PR to open it on GitHub, where you read it, review it, and answer the agent's review
in its threads. The dashboard's PR pages, its list of PRs outside the queue, and its review,
close and publish buttons are gone: label a PR on GitHub to queue it.
